# Drop your raw video here

1. Copy your raw video into this folder using Finder. Keep its original filename.
2. In Codex or your AI app of choice, say: **"I put a new video in drop-videos-here. Start a project for it."**
3. Your AI app puts it in a project: `workspace/projects/<project>/<video>/` and **moves** your video, unchanged, into its `source/` folder.

**Your video will not stay here.** After it is moved, a small note with the same name is left in this folder telling you where it went.

Finished videos appear in `workspace/finished-videos/`.
