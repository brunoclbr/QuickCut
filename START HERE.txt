START HERE — first-time setup (about 20 minutes, only once)

You need: a Mac and the Codex desktop app (or another AI app, such as Claude Code).

What gets downloaded during setup (your AI app asks before installing anything; about 1.5-2 GB in total):
- Free tools: Homebrew (if missing), Node.js, FFmpeg, HyperFrames (the video renderer,
  with its own browser) and whisper.cpp with a speech model (for captions from your voice).
- Free icons, animations and sound effects for the asset library (step 4).
- Optional: the ElevenLabs voiceover tools (step 6, about 100 MB). Voiceovers need an
  ElevenLabs account and use paid credits, always after your OK.
Full list with versions: README.md, section "What gets downloaded".

>> Whenever this guide says "Type", it means type it into the chat in Codex (or your AI app), just like sending a message. <<

1. Move this folder somewhere permanent, like Documents. Don't use it from inside the ZIP.
   After setup, don't rename or move it. If you ever need to: close Codex, rename or
   move it, open it again in Codex and type: "I renamed this folder from OLD NAME to
   NEW NAME, update everything."
2. In Codex, open this whole folder as a project.
   One brand or client = one copy of this folder. Using QuickCut for a second brand or
   client? Don't reuse this one: copy the original folder (or ask your AI app: "Make a clean
   copy of this workspace for <name>") and start again there.
3. Type: "Set up this video editing folder for me."
   It checks your Mac, installs the free tools it needs and makes a short test video.
   Say yes when it asks to install something. It also asks what to call this
   workspace (your brand or client).
4. Type: "Set up the asset library."
   It installs free icons, animations and sound effects, then asks you to download
   a few free extras in your browser:
   - Open Stickers: https://craftwork.gumroad.com/l/gxaylb (type 0 as the price)
   - Mixkit sound effects: https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/ (the free ones you need to download one by one, no account needed)
   Leave them in your Downloads folder. Don't unzip or move them.
   Then type: "hey i just downloaded "XYZ" and "ABC" can you move them from the downloads folder into this project and integrate properly?
5. Type: "Interview me about my editing style."
   Your answers are saved and used for every video.
6. Optional, AI voiceovers (paid, from ElevenLabs): type "Open the app.", go to
   Setup & preferences → ElevenLabs → Connect, and follow the steps there.
   Paste your key only in that box, never in the chat. Then just ask for a voiceover;
   it always shows you the script and the cost and waits for your OK.

Every video after that:
- Copy your video into  workspace/drop-videos-here
- Type: "I put a new video in drop-videos-here."
- To watch drafts and see where everything is, type: "Open the app."
- Your finished videos appear in  workspace/finished-videos

Your videos stay on your computer; nothing is uploaded. (Only if you use AI voiceovers,
the scripts you approve are sent to ElevenLabs to be spoken.)
If something goes wrong, just describe it to your AI app in plain words.
